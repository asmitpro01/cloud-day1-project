Have you ever wondered why we feel sleepy after a big lunch?

It is not just in your head. After you eat, your body sends extra blood to your stomach and gut to help digest the food.

A meal rich in carbohydrates also raises a hormone called insulin, which helps an amino acid named tryptophan reach the brain.

In the brain, tryptophan is turned into serotonin and then melatonin, the chemicals that tell your body it is time to rest.

On top of that, your natural body clock already dips in the early afternoon, so a heavy meal arrives right when you are least alert.

The fix is simple. Eat a lighter lunch, add some protein and vegetables, drink water, and take a short walk afterwards.

Next time the afternoon slump hits, you will know exactly what is going on inside you.
